# Catalog v2 review notes

Written by the adversarial reviewers of the Phase 2 data wave (2026-10-03). These are calls they would not change on their own. Each one lists the reviewer's recommendation. Clear errors were fixed directly in the records; these are the debatable ones. Mark each as accept, change or ignore, and the lead applies the result.

## Proposed citations (11)

These are not added to `data/sources.json` yet. Each needs a check that the paper exists and that it tested the movement it would back.

- Camara KD et al. (2016). An examination of muscle activation and power characteristics while performing the deadlift exercise with straight and hexagonal barbells. J Strength Cond Res 30(5):1183-1188. Would support 516 quads primary.
- Distefano LJ et al. (2009). Gluteal muscle activation during common therapeutic exercises. J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 39(7):532-540. Would support 536.
- Escamilla RF et al. (2002). An electromyographic analysis of sumo and conventional style deadlifts. Med Sci Sports Exerc 34(4):682-688. Would support 520 and 521 (author's proposal, I agree it exists).
- Escamilla RF, Lewis C, Bell D, Bramblet G, Daffron J, Lambert S, Pecson A, Imamura R, Paulos L, Andrews JR (2006). Electromyographic analysis of traditional and nontraditional abdominal exercises: implications for rehabilitation and training. Physical Therapy 86(5):656-671. Measured the ab wheel/roller and ab slide (rollout) along with crunch variants and reported rectus abdominis, obliques and latissimus activity. It would back 810/811 (and possibly 812) roles. The lead should verify the author list before adding it.
- Glass SC, Armstrong T (1997). Electromyographical activity of the pectoralis muscle during incline and decline bench presses. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research 11(3):163-167. Would back the sternal emphasis on 116 and 117.
- Marchetti PH, Uchida MC (2011). Effects of the pullover exercise on the pectoralis major and latissimus dorsi muscles as evaluated by EMG. Journal of Applied Biomechanics 27(4):380-384. It would back chest secondary on 326 (the study found pec major more active than lats), so keep confidence medium rather than raising lats-dominance claims.
- McCaw ST, Melrose DR (1999). Stance width and bar load effects on leg muscle activity during the parallel squat. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise 31(3):428-436. This would support higher adductor longus and glute max activity in wide-stance squats (421 sumo squat, and by analogy 426 and 427).
- Reinold MM et al. (2004). Electromyographic analysis of the rotator cuff and deltoid musculature during common shoulder external rotation exercises. J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 34(7):385-394. Would raise 211, 215 and 232 to confidence high, and informs deltoid roles in scaption (236).
- Saeterbakken AH, Fimland MS (2013). Effects of body position and loading modality on muscle activity and strength in shoulder presses. J Strength Cond Res 27(7):1824-1831. Backs standing vs seated and dumbbell vs barbell presses (217, 221, 235).
- Serner A et al. (2014). EMG evaluation of hip adduction exercises for soccer players. Br J Sports Med 48(14):1108-1114. Would support 538.
- Swinton PA et al. (2011). A biomechanical analysis of straight and hexagonal barbell deadlifts using submaximal loads. J Strength Cond Res 25(7):2000-2009. Would support 516 (more knee, less hip and lumbar moment).
