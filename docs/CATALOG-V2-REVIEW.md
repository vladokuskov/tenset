# Catalog v2 review notes

Written by the adversarial reviewers of the Phase 2 data wave (2026-10-03). These are calls they would not change on their own. Each one lists the reviewer's recommendation. Clear errors were fixed directly in the records; these are the debatable ones. Mark each as accept, change or ignore, and the lead applies the result.

## Proposed citations (11)

These are not added to `data/sources.json` yet. Each needs a check that the paper exists and that it tested the movement it would back.

- Camara KD et al. (2016). An examination of muscle activation and power characteristics while performing the deadlift exercise with straight and hexagonal barbells. J Strength Cond Res 30(5):1183-1188. Would support 516 quads primary. **Verified, added as `camara-2016`** (PMID 26840440). Measured vastus lateralis, biceps femoris and erector spinae; cited on 516.
- Distefano LJ et al. (2009). Gluteal muscle activation during common therapeutic exercises. J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 39(7):532-540. Would support 536. **Verified, added as `distefano-2009`** (PMID 19574661). Tested side-lying hip abduction and the lateral band walk; cited on 536 and 537.
- Escamilla RF et al. (2002). An electromyographic analysis of sumo and conventional style deadlifts. Med Sci Sports Exerc 34(4):682-688. Would support 520 and 521 (author's proposal, I agree it exists). **Verified, added as `escamilla-2002`** (PMID 11932579). Cited on 521, and on 520 by analogy.
- Escamilla RF, Lewis C, Bell D, Bramblet G, Daffron J, Lambert S, Pecson A, Imamura R, Paulos L, Andrews JR (2006). Electromyographic analysis of traditional and nontraditional abdominal exercises: implications for rehabilitation and training. Physical Therapy 86(5):656-671. Measured the ab wheel/roller and ab slide (rollout) along with crunch variants and reported rectus abdominis, obliques and latissimus activity. It would back 810/811 (and possibly 812) roles. The lead should verify the author list before adding it. **Corrected, added as `escamilla-2006`.** The author list above is wrong: Phys Ther 86(5):656-671 is Escamilla, Babb, DeWitt et al. (PMID 16649890). An earlier version of this note said it did not test a hand-held wheel; that was wrong, its Power Wheel roll-out is a kneeling rollout on wheel handles. The paper cited here is Escamilla, McTaggart, Fricklas et al. (2006), J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 36(2):45-57 (PMID 16494072), which tested the kneeling Ab Slide rollout and found it among the highest for rectus abdominis, oblique and latissimus activity. It is cited on 810 and 811; 811 is high, 810 (standing, by analogy) stays medium. 812 is not cited (oblique rollout was not tested).
- Glass SC, Armstrong T (1997). Electromyographical activity of the pectoralis muscle during incline and decline bench presses. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research 11(3):163-167. Would back the sternal emphasis on 116 and 117. **Verified, added as `glass-1997`** (Crossref doi:10.1519/00124278-199708000-00006, not in PubMed). Tested +30 and -15 degrees; cited on 116 and 117.
- Marchetti PH, Uchida MC (2011). Effects of the pullover exercise on the pectoralis major and latissimus dorsi muscles as evaluated by EMG. Journal of Applied Biomechanics 27(4):380-384. It would back chest secondary on 326 (the study found pec major more active than lats), so keep confidence medium rather than raising lats-dominance claims. **Verified, added as `marchetti-2011`** (PMID 21975179). Barbell pullover. Later dropped from 326's basis: it found the pec more active than the lats, while 326 keeps lats primary (open item below). 326 keeps anatomy-general, confidence medium.
- McCaw ST, Melrose DR (1999). Stance width and bar load effects on leg muscle activity during the parallel squat. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise 31(3):428-436. This would support higher adductor longus and glute max activity in wide-stance squats (421 sumo squat, and by analogy 426 and 427). **Verified, added as `mccaw-1999`** (PMID 10188748). Cited on 421.
- Reinold MM et al. (2004). Electromyographic analysis of the rotator cuff and deltoid musculature during common shoulder external rotation exercises. J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 34(7):385-394. Would raise 211, 215 and 232 to confidence high, and informs deltoid roles in scaption (236). **Verified, added as `reinold-2004`** (PMID 15296366). Tested side-lying and standing external rotation at 0 degrees; cited on 211, 215 and 232 (now high). Not cited on 236: it did not test a scaption raise.
- Saeterbakken AH, Fimland MS (2013). Effects of body position and loading modality on muscle activity and strength in shoulder presses. J Strength Cond Res 27(7):1824-1831. Backs standing vs seated and dumbbell vs barbell presses (217, 221, 235). **Verified, added as `saeterbakken-2013`** (PMID 23096062). Cited on 217, 221 and 235 (235 as the seated dumbbell condition).
- Serner A et al. (2014). EMG evaluation of hip adduction exercises for soccer players. Br J Sports Med 48(14):1108-1114. Would support 538. **Verified, added as `serner-2014`** (PMID 23511698). Tested the Copenhagen adduction; cited on 538.
- Swinton PA et al. (2011). A biomechanical analysis of straight and hexagonal barbell deadlifts using submaximal loads. J Strength Cond Res 25(7):2000-2009. Would support 516 (more knee, less hip and lumbar moment). **Verified, added as `swinton-2011`** (PMID 21659894). Cited on 516.
